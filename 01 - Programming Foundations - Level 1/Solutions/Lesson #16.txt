🔹 Set A:-
1️⃣ 6 ÷ 3 + 4 × 2
Division & Multiplication first (left to right):
6 \ 3 = 2
4 * 2 = 8
Expression becomes: 2 + 8
Final answer: 10

2️⃣ 6 × 4 − 12 ÷ 3 − 8
Multiplication & Division first:
6 * 4 = 24
12 \ 3 = 4
Expression becomes: 24 - 4 - 8
Subtract left to right:
24 - 4 = 20
20 - 8 = 12
Final answer: 12

3️⃣ 4 × 4 − 3 × 3 − 16 ÷ 4
Multiplication & Division first:
4 * 4 = 16
3 * 3 = 9
16 \ 4 = 4
Expression becomes: 16 - 9 - 4
Subtract left to right:
16 - 9 = 7
7 - 4 = 3
Final answer: 3

4️⃣ 20 − (3 × 23 − 5)

Subtract: 69 - 5 = 64
Outer expression: 20 - 64 = -44
Final answer: -44

5️⃣ (5 + 2)² − 9 × 3 + 2³
Parentheses first: (5 + 2) = 7
Exponents next: 7^2 = 49 and 2^3 = 8
Expression becomes: 49 - 9 * 3 + 8
Multiply: 9 * 3 = 27
Add and subtract left to right:
49 - 27 = 22
22 + 8 = 30
Final answer: 30

6️⃣ (7 − √9) × (4² − 3 + 1) (assuming 42 is 4^2)

Final answer: 160

🔹 Set B:-

🔹 Set C:-
1️⃣ (5 > 6 and 7=7) OR (1 Or 0)
First parentheses: (5 > 6 and 7=7) ==>> (False and True) ==>> False
Second parentheses: (1 Or 0) ==>> (True Or False) ==>> True
Full expression: False OR True
Result: True

2️⃣ NOT(5 > 6 and 7=7) OR (1 Or 0)
Inside NOT(...): (5 > 6 and 7=7) ==>> (False and True) ==>> False
Apply NOT: NOT(False) ==>> True
Second parentheses: (1 Or 0) ==>> True
Full expression: True OR True
Result: True

3️⃣ NOT(5 > 6 and 7=7) OR NOT (1 Or 0)
Left side: NOT(False and True) ==>> NOT(False) ==>> True
Right side: NOT(True Or False) ==>> NOT(True) ==>> False
Full expression: True OR False
Result: True

4️⃣ NOT(5 > 6 OR 7=7) AND NOT (1 Or 0)
Left side: 5 > 6 OR 7=7 ==>> False OR True ==>> True
Apply NOT: NOT(True) ==>> False
Right side: 1 Or 0 ==>> True
Apply NOT: NOT(True) ==>> False
Full expression: False AND False
Result: False

5️⃣ ((5>6 AND 7<=8) OR (8>1 and 4<=3)) AND True
First pair: 5>6 AND 7<=8 ==>> False AND True ==>> False
Second pair: 8>1 and 4<=3 ==>> True and False ==>> False
Combine inside large parentheses: False OR False ==>> False
Full expression: False AND True
Result: False

6️⃣ ( (5>6 AND NOT(7<=8)) AND (8>1 OR 4<=3) ) OR True
Since the entire grouped expression is connected to OR True at the end, any Boolean expression OR True automatically evaluates to True.
Checking step-by-step:
Left side: 5>6 AND NOT(7<=8) ==>> False AND NOT(True) ==>> False AND False ==>> False
Right side: 8>1 OR 4<=3 ==>> True OR False ==>> True
Combine inside: False AND True ==>> False
Outermost evaluation: False OR True
Result: True
